Research Overview

I am interested in developing provable algorithms and lower bounds for simple structures or phenomena that explain learning in interactive, adaptive, and modern machine learning systems. To be more precise:

  • Online learning & learning in adaptive environments. I am interested in online learning, learning dynamics in games, reinforcement learning.
  • Deep learning theory. I am interested in optimization and learning dynamics, model expressivity, and generative models such as diffusion models.
  • Sampling & statistical estimation. I am interested in a lot of algorithmic statistics related question, especially sampling and robustness.
  • Classical learning theory. I am interested in classical PAC learning theory or related style frameworks about sample complexity of learning, active learning, etc.

Across these areas, I tend to gravitate toward clean theoretical questions with an extremal or optimization flavor: sharp thresholds, minimax rates, optimality guarantees and lower bounds.
